# Human Oversight & Redress Framework (ISO 42001 Annex A.8.3 & EU AI Act Art. 14)

## 1. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Governance Principle
AI outputs generated by corporate prototypes serve strictly as **Decision Support Systems (DSS)**. Final decisions impacting personnel, compensation, or talent acquisition remain 100% under human managerial authority.

## 2. Escalation & Appeal Protocol
[ AI System Evaluation ] ➔ [ Risk Flag Triggered ] ➔ [ HR Manager Review ] ➔ [ Board / Appeals Review ]

1. **Automated Flagging:** Any candidate evaluation falling within borderlines or flagged for potential bias is isolated.
2. **Mandatory Human Intervention:** HR专員 must review the raw resume alongside AI reasoning before finalizing decisions.
3. **Redress Channel:** Applicants may request human re-evaluation under PCPD Data Access Rights.
