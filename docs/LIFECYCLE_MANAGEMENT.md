## 2. Change & Version Control Protocol
* **Major Version Updates (e.g., v1.0 ➔ v2.0):** Triggered by updates to core AI regulations (e.g., EU AI Act enforcement milestones or ISO 42001 revision). Requires full re-audit of `MODEL_CARD.md`.
* **Regression Testing:** Automated bench testing performed across 50 standardized HR governance scenarios prior to pushing updates to main branch.
